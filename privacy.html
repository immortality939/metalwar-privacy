Metal War - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy explains what the Metal War game ("the game", "we") collects and how it is used.

Email and personal details

The game does not ask for your email address, phone number, real name or any other personal details.

Online mode

If you play online, the game creates a random Player ID and a recovery code for your account. This ID and your game progress (character, level, items, gold, points, clan) are stored on our server so that you can continue playing and recover your account with your recovery code. Keep your recovery code private. If you lose it, we cannot recover your account.

Offline mode

In offline mode your progress is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.

Ads

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other device and usage information to show ads and measure them. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the services needed to run the game (our server host and Google AdMob).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Data deletion

To ask us to delete your online account data, contact us with your Player ID at the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
